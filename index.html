Storage speed shows up before the loading bar moves

Eka Putri · 28 October 2026 · 4-minute read

When my phone started taking forty seconds to reach a game lobby, I blamed the chipset. It was not the chipset. I copied the same three games to internal storage and to a microSD card, then timed the stages by hand.

Same 2022 mid-range phone, same games, two places to store them.
StageInternalmicroSD
Cold app launch9 s18 s
First level load12 s31 s
Texture pop-in after spawnrareconstant

The card was not broken. It was simply slow at the thing games actually do: thousands of small random reads, not one big sequential copy. A card advertising 92 MB/s sequential read still stalls when the engine asks for four kilobytes at a time across a thousand files.

Two things fixed most of it for me. Keep at least fifteen percent of internal storage free, because a full flash chip has nowhere to shuffle data and writes get slower. And move only screenshots and music to the card, never installed games.

If a game is slow to load on a phone that still plays it smoothly once you are inside, look at storage before you look at the processor.
